# Animation plan

## Performance loop v0

A six-pose looping study built around Freddie Mercury's instantly readable stage silhouette. The target is puppet energy, not frame-by-frame imitation of any specific copyrighted concert footage.

### Pose A — planted
Feet apart, torso forward, microphone near chest. Hold just long enough to establish the silhouette.

### Pose B — coil
Weight shifts onto the rear leg. Shoulder nearest the microphone drops slightly. Head turns a few degrees before the body catches up.

### Pose C — strike
Fast chest lift and shoulder snap. Microphone hand punches outward. The free arm counterbalances behind the torso.

### Pose D — crown
Brief heroic hold at maximum extension. Keep this the cleanest, most poster-like frame.

### Pose E — recoil
Elbow softens first, then shoulder and chest follow. Head lags a fraction to give the motion weight.

### Pose F — settle
Return past neutral by a small amount, then ease back to Pose A. This overshoot prevents the loop feeling robotic.

## Timing target
- 24 fps working timeline
- 28–36 frames total for the first loop
- strike should be fast, roughly 3–5 frames
- crown hold 2–3 frames
- recoil and settle use most of the remaining frames

## Secondary motion
Add only after the body loop reads clearly:
- tiny wrist follow-through on the microphone
- subtle moustache/cheek expression change rather than lip-sync
- vest/fabric drag of 1–2 frames
- slight knee compression on recoil

## Visual treatment
Start against pure black with a simplified stage-light rim so the pose reads at thumbnail size. Keep the figure stylised and graphic enough to animate cleanly.
