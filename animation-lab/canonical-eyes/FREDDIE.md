# Freddie cameo — lab v12

Ryan approved a moustache and half-mic stand with floating felt hands, no torso
or jacket. The concept image is reference only: runtime uses the byte-locked
original eyes and hand artwork, code-rendered props, and FreddieMotion state.

Five digits per hand is strict: four individually sampled front fingers plus
one separately sampled opposing thumb. Do not substitute generated three-finger
mittens. These are prop grips, not validated ASL or BSL poses; future signing
needs language-specific motion/anatomy review.

Select Freddie on the last lab row, or launch with `--ei sign 4`. Freeze and
slow-review controls work as for the signs. FreddieMotion uses the host's logical
clock, enters, struts/tilts the mic and settles at nine seconds. Reduced motion
shows a held pose. Returning to a sign or eye clip exits Freddie mode.

The Queen-track Easter egg remains a future production integration: this demo
does not read music metadata, play audio, or control playback. Preserve existing
notification privacy, media ownership and lifecycle rules when transplanting.
