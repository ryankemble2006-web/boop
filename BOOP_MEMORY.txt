WHY REFRESH BROKE
The lab removed its only metadata/playback callback while the same active session
passed through temporary NONE/STOPPED/SKIPPING states. Without that callback it
could miss the next song until Activity restart. Observer lifetime must follow
the live session token, separately from which states can display a music panel.
Retain observation through transient states; still clear old content. Detach on
actual removal, permission loss and the lab becoming inactive. Do not add polling
or recreate the Activity to hide a broken subscription.
