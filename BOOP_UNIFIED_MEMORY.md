Keep focused functional/compile/lint/signature/package/integrity/security checks and immediate upload. Historical tests remain but are not selected. Do not re-enable removed acceptance stages silently.
- Foreground wireless charging permits continuous wake; undocked is tap-to-talk. Preserve mic ownership; no always-on handheld workaround, automatic installs or grants.
- Hands/2.5D puppetry, later HA sensor rails and AC teaching remain deferred. Preserve media, voice, launcher, permissions and signing.
