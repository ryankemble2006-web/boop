## Preserved product and privacy behavior

- BOOP is an expressive, honest puppet, not a claim of sentience. Existing blue/cyan eyes, five-digit yellow hands, approved headphones, accessibility direction and character geometry remain locked. The 2.5D/hand/AC ideas remain deferred.
- Home Assistant is the local authority. Immediate home/media control stays local; use real entity identifiers and preserve explicit room intent. Never quietly operate a similar device in another room. Automations/scripts/scenes retain the user-facing name Routines and their distinct execution semantics.
- Shield shown Home/settings inventory is fail-closed to the selected area. Read HA target expansion and registry metadata, including device-supplied area associations; filter locally as defense in depth. No moving, renaming or rewriting HA configuration. Existing legitimate controls remain available.
- Undocked handheld Wall mode is tap-to-talk with continuous wake capture disarmed. Wireless charging is the foreground listening dock signal. TTS/tap/settings/lifecycle handoffs retain microphone ownership safeguards. Shield's overlay remains non-focusable, non-touchable, without microphone or HA socket.
- Docked eyes can sleep. Available proximity events can prompt a brief camera peek, not continuous idle image recognition. Proximity is not whole-room motion sensing; actual hardware effectiveness remains to be tested. Mirror explicitly uses continuous front-camera preview only while requested. Horizontal INSIDE/OUTSIDE rails reserve future sensor data; do not invent mappings.
- Three-second Chat mode selection, local-first routing, accepted hue/sleep/blink/landscape behavior, launcher behavior and permanent signing are preserved. Provider keys remain outside Android; no secrets, private device data or raw diagnostic dumps belong in this public repository.
