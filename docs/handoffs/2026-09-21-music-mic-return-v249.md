## White-circle follow-up: identified, not removed

Ryan clarified that the white circle appears at the bottom centre immediately after the physical microphone press, before BOOP fills the screen. Live Shield inspection identified SystemUI's AssistPreviewPanel window and the installed SystemUI resources assist_orb_color (white) and assist_orb_size (144dp). The Shield runs Android11. This is the system assistant launch orb, outside BOOP's Activity; direct Activity launches bypass it. No supported per-assistant suppression switch was found on this firmware. Resource overlays require system/signature authority when the target does not expose suitable overlayable resources. No overlay, key remapping, assistant-role change or alternative voice service was installed or enabled. The existing microphone route and accepted timing fix remain in place; circle removal is unresolved.
